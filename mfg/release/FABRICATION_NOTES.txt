GPSDO DDMTD FRONT END  --  fabrication notes
============================================

BOARD
  Outline ............ 207.00 x 180.00 mm, rectangular, no cutouts, no slots
  Copper layers ...... 4   (F.Cu / In1.Cu / In2.Cu / B.Cu)
  Suggested stackup .. 1.6 mm FR-4, 1 oz outer, 0.5 oz inner  (JLC04161H-7628)
  Surface finish ..... ENIG preferred (lead-free HASL acceptable)
  Soldermask ......... any colour; silkscreen white
  Impedance control .. NOT required.  See the note below.

WHAT IS ON EACH LAYER
  F.Cu      components, most routing, and a GND pour
  In1.Cu    solid GND plane
  In2.Cu    the helper-clock distribution, as stripline between In1 and B.Cu
  B.Cu      solid GND plane; the Raspberry Pi socket mounts on this side

MINIMUMS ACTUALLY USED  (all well inside JLCPCB's 4-layer capability)
  Minimum track ...... 0.25 mm
  Track widths used .. 0.25, 0.30, 0.35, 0.50
  Via pad/drill ...... 0.60/0.30 mm      (595 vias)
  Minimum via drill .. 0.3 mm
  Pad drill sizes .... 1.00, 1.20, 1.50, 1.70, 3.20
  Minimum pad drill .. 1.0 mm
  Closest SMT pad to the board edge .. 1.19 mm  (FB5009)
      An ASSEMBLY number, not a fabrication one, and no KiCad rule checks it:
      `copper_edge_clearance` is about copper, not about whether a nozzle and
      a clamp can both reach.  If it is inside your rail allowance the panel
      needs a break-away rail -- PLEASE SAY SO RATHER THAN SHIFTING THE PART.
      Everything nearer the edge than this is through hole (the SMA jacks, the
      screw terminals, the barrel jack, the two bench posts and the 2x20
      socket) and is hand soldered afterwards.

IMPEDANCE
  No controlled-impedance service is required.  The helper traces on In2.Cu
  are drawn as nominal 50 ohm stripline against the two adjacent ground
  planes, but every one of them is source-terminated at its driver and none is
  longer than ~120 mm at 10 MHz, where the electrical length is a small
  fraction of a wavelength.  Build to the standard stackup.

ASSEMBLY
  Surface mount, TOP SIDE ONLY.  Nothing on the bottom is to be placed:
  the only bottom-side part is the Raspberry Pi socket, which is through hole
  and is hand soldered afterwards.
  See GPSDO-PYNQ-Z2_bom.csv and GPSDO-PYNQ-Z2_cpl.csv.
  Parts NOT to be fitted are listed in excluded_from_assembly.csv and appear
  in neither file -- please do not add them back.

WHAT TO SET ON THE JLCPCB ORDER FORM
  Base material ............ FR-4
  Layers ................... 4
  Dimensions ............... 207 x 180 mm, 1 piece, single design
  PCB thickness ............ 1.6 mm
  Impedance control ........ No
  Via covering ............. Tented
  Min via hole size/diameter 0.3 mm / 0.6 mm  (matches this design exactly)
  Board outline tolerance .. regular (+/-0.2 mm)
  Surface finish ........... ENIG  (lead-free HASL is acceptable)
  Remove order number ...... "Specify a location" or "No" -- there is room in
                             the lower-left margin below the title block
  PCB assembly ............. Yes, TOP side, 1 boards
  Tooling holes ............ Added by JLCPCB
  Confirm parts placement .. Yes  -- NOT OPTIONAL, see ROTATION below

ROTATION  (round 33)
  KiCad's footprint zero-orientation and JLCPCB's feeder zero-orientation are
  not the same, and the offset is a property of JLCPCB's library.  Two
  placement files are supplied:
    GPSDO-PYNQ-Z2_cpl.csv ............... KiCad-native rotations
    GPSDO-PYNQ-Z2_cpl_jlc_corrected.csv . the same, corrected.  UPLOAD THIS ONE.
  Round 28 built the corrections from the JLCKicadTools table.  Round 33
  rebuilt them from YOUR OWN placement preview, which is the only authority,
  and two of the five were wrong -- one of them on 59 parts:
    MSOP-10_3x3mm_P0.5mm                 +270  x1    U3001
        on U3001, pad 1 is at the north-east corner of its own body
        pin 1 dot
    Oscillator_SMD_Fox_FT5H_5.0x3.2mm    +270  x1    X3001
        on X3001, pad 1 is at the north-east corner of its own body
        pin 1 is the NORTH-EAST pad; a 0.8 mm dot is printed beside it
        from round 35, because this footprint ships with no pin-1 mark of
        its own
    SOIC-8_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm             +270  x9    U5001, U6001
        on U5001, pad 1 is at the north-west corner of its own body
        pin 1 dot
    SOT-223-3_TabPin2                    +180  x3    Q2001
        on Q2001, pad 1 is at the north-west corner of its own body
        tab = drain, the wide pad
    SOT-23-5                             +180  x59   U102 + 58 others
        on U102, pad 1 is at the north-west corner of its own body
        pin 1 = the D input
    SOT-23-6                             +270  x1    U5009
        on U5009, pad 1 is at the north-west corner of its own body
        pin 1 dot
    D_SMA                                   0  x4    D2001
        cathode band points south and the silkscreen's closed side is
        south, so it is already right -- correcting it would be the bug
  Still confirm them in the preview before approving.  Two footprints on this
  board have never been checked against a preview at all, both polarised:
    D2003 (5.6V 5W Zener, SMB)   cathode band
    D6001 (PESD5V0S1BA, SOD-323) cathode band
  If a preview still disagrees, upload the native file instead and tell the
  generator: the table is mfg/parts_map.py, JLC_ROTATION, keyed on the exact
  footprint name.

PIN 1, PART BY PART  (round 35)
  Where the preview's pin-1 dot must land.  North is the screw-terminal edge
  of the board, south is the Raspberry Pi socket edge.  Every one of these is
  computed from the board, and the same arithmetic has been checked against
  KiCad's own pcbnew for all 458 placements.
    D_SMA  (4)
      rot  -90 -> pin 1 north       x3   D2002, D2004, D2005
      rot   90 -> pin 1 south       x1   D2001
    D_SMB  (1)
      rot  180 -> pin 1 east        x1   D2003
    D_SOD-323  (1)
      rot    0 -> pin 1 west        x1   D6001
    MSOP-10_3x3mm_P0.5mm  (1)
      rot  -90 -> pin 1 north-east  x1   U3001
    Oscillator_SMD_Fox_FT5H_5.0x3.2mm  (1)
      rot  -90 -> pin 1 north-east  x1   X3001
    SOIC-8_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm  (9)
      rot    0 -> pin 1 north-west  x6   U5001, U5003, U5005, U5007, U5008 ... +1 more
      rot  180 -> pin 1 south-east  x3   U5002, U5004, U5006
    SOT-223-3_TabPin2  (3)
      rot    0 -> pin 1 north-west  x3   Q2001, Q2002, Q2003
    SOT-23-5  (59)
      rot  -90 -> pin 1 north-east  x6   U3002, U3003, U3004, U3005, U3006 ... +1 more
      rot    0 -> pin 1 north-west  x27  U101, U102, U103, U104, U301 ... +22 more
      rot   90 -> pin 1 south-west  x2   U2001, U2002
      rot  180 -> pin 1 south-east  x24  U201, U202, U203, U204, U401 ... +19 more
    SOT-23-6  (1)
      rot    0 -> pin 1 north-west  x1   U5009
    80 polarised parts; the other 378 are unpolarised two-terminal passives.
  Every polarised footprint above carries a filled pin-1 triangle of its own
  EXCEPT the FT5H oscillator, which ships with none; round 35 prints a 0.8 mm
  dot beside its pin 1.  The three diodes mark the CATHODE, which is pad 1,
  as the closed end of the silkscreen outline.
